## What it is

Curated App Store screenshot gallery for ASO and mobile UI, filterable by genre (Food & Drink), tone of voice, style (Flat Design, Minimalist, Photographic), color and font style. Part of ogimage.gallery.

## Best patterns

- ReciMe is filed under Food & Drink with tags Friendly, Informative, Creative, Minimalist, Flat Design, Orange/White/Black, Sans-Serif.
- Peer set surfaced on the same page: Wecipe (Recipe Importer), Mr. Cook, Pestle, Crumb, Jow, Too Good To Go: useful for seeing how import-first recipe apps sell the paste/share flow in 5 to 8 frames.
- Filters by tone (Friendly, Supportive) and style (Minimalist, Photographic) help pick a Play Store look that matches the soft UI.

## Compose adaptation

Use for the Play Store screenshot set and short headline copy per frame (paste link → review → cook). In-app: borrow the one-sentence benefit line under the Discover paste strip, set in `bodyMedium` Muted.

## Do not copy

Store frames are marketing composites (device mockups, big headlines); do not port their gradient or 3D backgrounds into app surfaces.
