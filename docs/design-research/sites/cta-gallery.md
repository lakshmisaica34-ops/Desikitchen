## What it is

Curated CTAs by type: Button, Call-to-Buy, Download, Form, Modal / Pop-up, Newsletter, Pricing / Subscription; filters by industry and mode.

## Best patterns

- Form CTAs pair a single input with a single pill button: the same shape as a paste-link strip.
- Pricing / Subscription CTAs show one primary plan action with a quiet secondary.
- Industry and mode filters make it quick to see how a single action is framed.

## Compose adaptation

Paste strip = `SoftCard` containing `SoftField` (trailing paste icon) + one `PillButton` labelled `Import`. Paywall = one `PillButton` (`Get Plus`) + `SoftOutlinePill` (`Not now`).

Skip pop-up modals on launch, countdowns and newsletter captures.
