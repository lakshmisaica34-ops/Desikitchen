I haven't bought a jar of salsa in years

Ingredients:
6 Roma tomatoes
1 white onion
2 jalapeños
1 bunch cilantro
Juice of 2 limes
1 tsp salt
Dice the tomatoes, onion and jalapeños into small pieces.
Finely chop the cilantro, stems and all.
Mix everything in a big bowl with the lime juice and salt.
Spoon it into a clean jar and keep it in the fridge for up to a week.
