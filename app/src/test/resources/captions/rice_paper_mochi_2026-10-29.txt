Mango rice paper mochi 🥭 only 65 kcal each! Save this for later 👇

Ingredients:
- 4 rice paper sheets
- 4 tbsp Greek yogurt
- 1 ripe mango, diced
- 1 tsp honey
Dip 1 rice paper sheet in warm water for 5 seconds.
Now place it flat on a plate and let it soften for a minute.
Add 1 tbsp yogurt and a few mango cubes in the centre.
Repeat to make another layer with a second rice paper sheet.
Now fold it nicely and tuck the edges underneath.
Freeze for an hour, and enjoy your mochi!

Nutrition (per mochi)
65 kcal Calories
2 g Protein
12 g Carbs
1 g Fats
